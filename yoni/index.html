Yoni — Privacy Policy

Русская версия

Effective date: 4 August 2026

Yoni ("the app") is a personal diary and calendar app for keeping track of your cycle and everyday wellbeing. This policy explains what the app does with the notes you keep in it. The short version: your data stays on your phone. We do not collect, transmit, sell or share it. We have no servers and no access to anything you enter.

1. Who we are

Yoni is developed and published by the Yoni team ("we").

Contact: softerica.softerica@gmail.com

2. What you keep in the app

The app is a private diary: you decide what to write down. Entries can include:

  • cycle dates and intensity;
  • morning temperature readings and the time you took them;
  • personal daily observations;
  • intimacy notes;
  • how you feel, mood, weight, wellbeing readings and free-text notes.

All of these entries are stored exclusively in a local database on your device. It is never uploaded, synchronised or transmitted anywhere by the app. We cannot see it, and no third party receives it from us.

3. Data we collect

None. The app contains:

  • no analytics or tracking SDKs;
  • no advertising and no advertising identifiers;
  • no user accounts or registration;
  • no network transmission of your entries.

Because we collect nothing, there is nothing to share: the entries you make are never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, and never shared with or transferred to any third party, data broker or advertising network — by us or by anyone acting on our behalf. The app does not create user profiles and does not link your entries to any identifier.

4. Permissions the app uses

PermissionPurpose
NotificationsLocal reminders you set (e.g. to take your temperature). Scheduled on the device only.
Biometrics / device credentialsOptional app lock (PIN or fingerprint/face). Biometric verification is performed by your device's operating system; the app never sees or stores your biometric data.
Camera flash (flashlight)The optional flashlight button on the temperature card, for reading a thermometer in the dark. The camera itself is never used; no images are captured.
File access (via system file picker)Only when you explicitly export a backup/CSV or restore a backup. Files go where you choose; nothing is sent to us.

5. Backups and exports

When you export a backup or CSV file, the file is created on your device and handed to the app you choose (file manager, e-mail, cloud drive, etc.). From that moment the file is governed by the privacy policy of the destination you selected. Protect exported files as you would any sensitive document.

Android cloud backup. The app opts out of Android Auto Backup, so your diary database is not copied to Google Drive or to any other Google backup service. The only way your entries leave the device is an export you perform yourself.

6. Purchases

If you buy Yoni Premium, the payment is processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment details. Google provides us only with an anonymous confirmation that the purchase is valid, and the transaction is covered by Google's own privacy policy. Purchase status is not linked to anything you write in the app.

7. Children

The app is intended for an adult audience and is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction).

8. Data retention, your rights and control

Your entries are kept on your device for as long as you keep them — we operate no servers, so we retain nothing and our retention period is zero. Because all data is stored locally, you have direct control:

  • Access / portability: use Settings → Export to get a full copy of your data at any time.
  • Deletion: use Settings → Clear all data, or simply uninstall the app — this permanently removes the local database.
  • We hold no copy, so there is nothing for us to delete or hand over on our side. You can still write to the address above with any privacy question.

9. Security

Data is stored in the app's private storage area, isolated by the operating system from other apps. You can additionally enable the app lock (PIN/biometrics) in Settings. Keep your device protected with a screen lock for best security.

10. Informational use only

Yoni is a lifestyle and self-tracking app. It is not a medical device, does not provide medical advice or diagnosis, and is not a method of contraception. Calendar predictions and charts are informational estimates based on what you type in, nothing more. For anything concerning your health, consult a qualified professional.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy (for example, when new features are added), the updated version will be published at the same address with a new effective date. Continued use of the app after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
