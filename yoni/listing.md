```
Yoni is a calm, private diary for your cycle and everyday wellbeing.

No account. No sign-up. No cloud. Nothing you write ever leaves your phone.

Most diary apps ask you to register, then quietly sync your most personal notes to someone else's server. Yoni does not. It has no servers at all — there is simply nowhere for your entries to go.

WHAT YOU CAN KEEP IN IT

• Cycle calendar — mark your days and see the whole month at a glance
• Morning temperature — log readings and watch the curve build up over time
• Daily observations — whatever you like to keep an eye on
• Mood and how you feel — a quick tap, or a longer note
• Weight and other everyday readings
• Intimacy notes
• Free-text notes for anything that doesn't fit a field

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

• No account, no registration, no email required
• No analytics, no tracking SDKs, no advertising IDs
• No ads
• Nothing is uploaded, synced or shared — the app works fully offline
• Optional app lock with PIN or fingerprint
• Cloud backup is switched off, so your diary is never copied to Drive

YOUR DATA IS YOURS

Export everything to a backup file or CSV whenever you want, and keep it where you choose. Clear all entries in one tap, or just uninstall — the local database goes with it. We hold no copy, so there is nothing for anyone to request from us.

A CALENDAR THAT LOOKS PLEASANT

Soft colours, a clean month view, light and dark themes, and reminders you set yourself — so the app nudges you only when you asked it to.

PREMIUM

Yoni is free to use. An optional one-time Premium unlocks extra charts and customisation. Payment is handled by Google Play; we never see your payment details.

GOOD TO KNOW

Yoni is a personal diary and self-tracking app for informational use. It is not a medical device, does not give medical advice or diagnosis, and is not a method of contraception. Calendar predictions are simple estimates based on the dates you enter yourself. For anything concerning your health, talk to a qualified professional.

Privacy policy: https://softerica-softerica.github.io/app-policy/yoni/
Questions: softerica.softerica@gmail.com
```
