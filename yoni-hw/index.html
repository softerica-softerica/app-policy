Yoni — Privacy Policy

Русская версия

Effective date: 17 July 2026

Yoni ("the app") is a menstrual-cycle tracking application. This policy explains what data the app handles and how. The short version: your data stays on your phone. We do not collect, transmit, sell or share it. We have no servers and no access to anything you enter.

1. Who we are

Yoni is developed and published by the Yoni team ("we").

Contact: softerica.softerica@gmail.com

2. Data you enter into the app

The app lets you record health-related information, including:

  • menstrual cycle dates and flow;
  • basal body temperature and measurement time;
  • cervical mucus and cervix observations;
  • intercourse records;
  • symptoms, mood, weight, blood pressure and free-text notes.

All of this data is stored exclusively in a local database on your device. It is never uploaded, synchronised or transmitted anywhere by the app. We cannot see it, and no third party receives it from us.

3. Data we collect

None. The app contains:

  • no analytics or tracking SDKs;
  • no advertising;
  • no user accounts or registration;
  • no network transmission of your entries.

4. Permissions the app uses

PermissionPurpose
NotificationsLocal reminders you set (e.g. to take your temperature). Scheduled on the device only.
Biometrics / device credentialsOptional app lock (PIN or fingerprint/face). Biometric verification is performed by your device's operating system; the app never sees or stores your biometric data.
Camera flash (flashlight)The optional flashlight button on the temperature card, for reading a thermometer in the dark. The camera itself is never used; no images are captured.
File access (via system file picker)Only when you explicitly export a backup/CSV or restore a backup. Files go where you choose; nothing is sent to us.

5. Backups and exports

When you export a backup or CSV file, the file is created on your device and handed to the app you choose (file manager, e-mail, cloud drive, etc.). From that moment the file is governed by the privacy policy of the destination you selected. Protect exported files as you would any sensitive document.

6. Purchases

If you buy Yoni Premium, the payment is processed entirely by the app store platform (e.g. Huawei AppGallery / Huawei IAP). We do not receive or store your payment details. The platform provides us only with an anonymous confirmation that the purchase is valid. The platform's own privacy policy applies to the payment process.

7. Children

The app is intended for users of reproductive age and is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction).

8. Your rights and control

Because all data is stored locally on your device, you have direct control:

  • Access / portability: use Settings → Export to get a full copy of your data at any time.
  • Deletion: use Settings → Clear all data, or simply uninstall the app — this permanently removes the local database.
  • We hold no copy, so there is nothing for us to delete or hand over on our side.

9. Security

Data is stored in the app's private storage area, isolated by the operating system from other apps. You can additionally enable the app lock (PIN/biometrics) in Settings. Keep your device protected with a screen lock for best security.

10. Medical disclaimer

Yoni is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or contraception guarantees. Predictions and analytics are informational estimates based on the data you enter. Always consult a healthcare professional for medical decisions.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy (for example, when new features are added), the updated version will be published at the same address with a new effective date. Continued use of the app after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
